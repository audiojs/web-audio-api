This demonstrates the missing fundamental illusion, also called virtual pitch: it plays only the upper harmonics of a tone and omits the fundamental, and the ear still reconstructs the implied pitch. Listen in the browser, or render the harmonic complex from the Node CLI to inspect it in an editor or an FFT.
